About me

By day I am a Badminton England Level 1 coach, coaching for the last 3 years. I've been playing badminton for 6 years, but honestly I get more excited about coaching and supporting kids coming up in the sport than I do about my own game. This site is where I keep the training plans, tools, and photos/videos I use on and off court.
